# Privacy Policy for PowerPilot **Last updated: October 7, 2026** PowerPilot ("the App") is a network utility and automation application that provides features such as Wake-on-LAN, network diagnostics, network tools, device discovery, and network automation. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use PowerPilot. ## 1. Information We Collect PowerPilot does not collect or sell personal information. The App may process network-related information on your device in order to provide its functionality. This may include: * IP addresses * MAC addresses * Device names or hostnames * Network ports and connection information * Other information discovered from devices on your local network This information is used locally on your device for features such as network discovery, diagnostics, connectivity testing, and Wake-on-LAN. ## 2. How We Use Information Information processed by PowerPilot is used solely to provide the features of the App, including: * Discovering devices on your local network * Sending Wake-on-LAN packets * Testing network connectivity and ports * Performing network diagnostics * Managing network automation tasks configured by you PowerPilot does not use this information for advertising or user profiling. ## 3. Data Sharing PowerPilot does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The App does not send discovered local-network device information to a remote server operated by the developer. Network communications initiated by you may of course be sent to the devices or services you choose to interact with using the App. ## 4. Data Storage PowerPilot may store application settings and configuration data locally on your device so that your preferences and configured devices can be retained. The developer does not have access to this locally stored information. ## 5. Network Permissions PowerPilot may request Android network-related permissions required for its functionality. These permissions may allow the App to communicate with devices on your local network, discover network devices, or send network packets. PowerPilot uses these permissions only for the network functionality provided by the App. ## 6. Third-Party Services PowerPilot does not currently use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal information. If third-party services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. ## 7. Children's Privacy PowerPilot is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 8. Security PowerPilot is designed to process network information locally on your device whenever possible. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee absolute security. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the App or its functionality. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. ## 10.
