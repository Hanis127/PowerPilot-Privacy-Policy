# Privacy Policy for PowerPilot **Last updated: October 23, 2026** PowerPilot is a local-network utility and automation application that provides Wake-on-LAN, device discovery, network diagnostics, network tools, device monitoring, and network automation features. This Privacy Policy explains how information is processed when you use PowerPilot. ## 1. Data Controller For the purposes of applicable data-protection law, the data controller is: **Jan Prihoda** **Country:** Czech Republic **Email:** powerpilotapp@gmail.com PowerPilot does not require users to create an account with the developer. ## 2. Information Processed by the App PowerPilot processes information that you enter or that the App discovers on your local network. Depending on the features you use, this may include: - IP addresses - MAC addresses - Device names and hostnames - Broadcast addresses - Network addresses and subnet information - Network ports and service information - URLs configured for readiness checks - Device availability and connectivity results - Wake-on-LAN configuration - Scheduled Wake rules - Wake on Arrival rules - Notification and automation preferences - Records of Wake attempts and related activity This information is processed to provide the functionality requested by you. ## 3. Local Processing and Storage Saved devices, network configuration, automation rules, application settings, and activity history are stored locally on your device. PowerPilot does not transmit discovered local-network device information to a server operated by the developer. Local data remains on the device until you delete the relevant device, rule, or history within the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Depending on your Android device and system settings, Android or a device manufacturer may include some application data in a device backup or device-transfer process. Those services are controlled by the relevant platform or device provider rather than by PowerPilot. ## 4. How Information Is Used PowerPilot processes information for the following purposes: - Discovering devices and services on your local network - Saving device configurations selected by you - Sending Wake-on-LAN packets - Testing device availability - Testing TCP ports and other network connectivity - Performing subnet calculations and network diagnostics - Running Scheduled Wake rules - Running Wake on Arrival rules - Displaying notifications requested by you - Maintaining local activity history - Providing and restoring PowerPilot Pro entitlement PowerPilot does not use local-network information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or sale to third parties. ## 5. Legal Bases for Processing Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, information is processed on the following bases: - **Performance of a contract or action requested by you:** processing necessary to provide the device, network, automation, and purchase features you choose to use. - **Legitimate interests:** responding to support requests, maintaining the security and reliability of the App, and investigating technical problems. - **Legal obligations:** retaining information where required by applicable accounting, tax, consumer-protection, or other law. Local-network processing is initiated through your use and configuration of the App. ## 6. Network Communications PowerPilot communicates with devices and addresses selected or discovered on the local network. These communications may include: - Wake-on-LAN packets - Ping or other reachability checks - TCP connection attempts - HTTP or HTTPS readiness checks - Local-network discovery requests These communications are necessary to provide the network functionality requested by you. Information sent to a device or service is received and handled by the operator of that device or service. You should use PowerPilot only on networks and devices that you own or are authorized to access. ## 7. Google Play and PowerPilot Pro PowerPilot offers an optional lifetime PowerPilot Pro purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payment information and the purchase transaction under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card or bank-account details. PowerPilot receives or queries purchase and entitlement information needed to: - Display the localized product and price - Start the Google Play purchase flow - Confirm whether a purchase is pending or completed - Acknowledge eligible purchases - Unlock PowerPilot Pro - Restore previous purchases Google may make order, purchase, and transaction information available through Google Play systems for purchase administration, fraud prevention, customer support, accounting, and legal compliance. For information about Google's processing, consult Google's Privacy Policy and Google Play terms. ## 8. Support Communications If you contact the developer by email, the developer may process: - Your email address - Your name, if included - The contents of your message - Attachments that you choose to send - Technical information that you voluntarily provide - Subsequent correspondence concerning the request Support correspondence is used only to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, protect the App, or comply with legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, private keys, or other unnecessary sensitive information in a support request. Support correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request and maintain an appropriate record, unless a longer period is required by law. ## 9. Data Sharing The developer does not sell or rent personal data. Information may be processed by or disclosed to: - **Google Play**, for app distribution, purchase processing, licensing, entitlement verification, fraud prevention, and purchase restoration. - **Email service providers**, when you contact the developer for support. - **Public authorities or professional advisers**, where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims. - **Devices and services selected by you**, when PowerPilot performs the local-network operation you requested. PowerPilot does not include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics services. ## 10. International Data Transfers Local-network information processed by PowerPilot is not intentionally transferred to a server operated by the developer. Google and other service providers may process information in countries outside the European Economic Area. Their processing and transfer safeguards are governed by their respective privacy policies and contractual or legal arrangements. ## 11. Data Retention and Deletion PowerPilot retains local application data until you: - Delete a saved device or automation rule - Clear activity history - Clear the App's storage through Android settings - Uninstall the App You can delete most configuration and history directly within the App. Purchase records are managed through Google Play and may be retained by Google or the developer where necessary for entitlement restoration, transaction administration, fraud prevention, accounting, or legal compliance. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for support, security, record-keeping, or legal obligations. ## 12. Security PowerPilot is designed to keep saved device and local-network information on the device and to avoid transmitting that information to developer- operated servers. Reasonable technical measures are used to reduce unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no device, network, storage system, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for securing your Android device and local network and for using PowerPilot only with authorized devices. ## 13.
